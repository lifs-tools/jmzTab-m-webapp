What it is

The HUPO-PSI standard for reporting the results of mass-spectrometry metabolomics and lipidomics: one tab-separated text file with the study's metadata, the identified molecules with their quantities, and the features and evidence behind them.

What it is for

Depositing results in public repositories, exchanging them between tools, and reanalysing published studies without the software that produced them.

Who uses it

Researchers whose software exports mzTab-M or who prepare their results for a repository, the tools that write and read it, and the repositories that accept it, LipidCompass among them.

mzTab-M and jmzTab-m

mzTab-M is the HUPO-PSI tab-separated exchange format for quantitative results of mass spectrometry based metabolomics experiments. jmzTab-m is its Java reference implementation; this service uses it to parse and validate your files.

mzTab 1.0 validation was retired in version 2.0.0.
